Risk-free rate is the return on a default-free instrument with a duration matching the cash flows being discounted — in practice, the 10-year US Treasury yield. Beta measures how much the stock moves with the broader market; β = 1 means it moves in lockstep, β > 1 means it amplifies market moves, β < 1 means it dampens them. ERP is the historical extra return investors have earned for bearing equity risk over Treasuries.
