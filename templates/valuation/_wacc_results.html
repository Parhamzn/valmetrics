The equity and debt weights are based on market values, not book values — that's the theoretically correct approach because investors price securities at market, not at historical cost. We use the firm's market capitalization for equity and (as a practical shortcut) book debt for debt; this is acceptable unless the firm's bonds trade at a large discount to par. The after-tax cost of debt reflects that interest is tax-deductible, which is why debt is "cheaper" than equity.
