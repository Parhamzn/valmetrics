WACC is the firm's blended cost of capital, weighting equity and debt by their market values. It's the discount rate used in firm-level DCF valuations (for free cash flow to the firm). Reducing WACC by 1 percentage point typically increases DCF fair value by 15-25%, so getting WACC right matters a lot.
