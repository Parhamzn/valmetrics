How to read this

The reverse DCF holds every other assumption (margin, WACC, reinvestment, terminal growth) fixed and asks the model what revenue growth rate would make the DCF value equal the market price. If that implied growth looks higher than the company can plausibly sustain, the market is pricing the stock optimistically; if it looks negative or trivially low, the market may be pricing in trouble.
