ROIC measures how efficiently a company turns invested capital into operating profit. Compared to WACC it shows whether the business creates or destroys value. Sustained ROIC > WACC by a wide margin is the hallmark of a competitive moat (Buffett: "the only thing that matters in the long run").

Benchmark

Formula:
NOPAT = EBIT × (1 − tax)
IC = Equity + LT Debt + ST Debt − Cash
ROIC = NOPAT / IC
